This non-secret inventory is generated by `tools/sandbox roles`. It groups all sandbox identities by purpose and never contains private keys.

- Sandbox domain: `sandbox.example.com`
- Ethereum settlement network: Sepolia (`11155111`)
- L2 chain ID: `1900000001`
- Role-set fingerprint: `GENERATED_SHA256`

## 1. Chain deployment and governance

The ecosystem deployer, ecosystem governor, and chain owner are funded automatically from the sandbox funding wallet.

## 3. Sandbox funding

> Customer action: send exactly **1 Sepolia ETH** to the sandbox funding wallet.

## 4. Service and passive accounts

The fee account, Watchdog, and dormant optional-service wallets appear here. Optional profiles are not covered by the core 1 ETH guarantee.
